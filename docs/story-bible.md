## 1. The Full Three-Act Arc

### Logline

> *A traveller arrives in a ruined village the night it is unmade — and discovers the creature beneath it is the god who built the world, still grieving something it cannot name.*

### Central conflict

**Creation and destruction are the same act.**

The power that raised Vaelthar block by block can also unmake it. The question Auren must answer by the end is not *"can I kill the monster?"* but:

> **Can something that has forgotten how to create be reminded — or must it be destroyed?**

### The villain: The Hollow

| | |
|---|---|
| **What it looks like** | A massive presence nesting below Edhari. It takes villagers, crumbles stone, and leaves ash falling like snow. |
| **What it is** | The last of the **Shapers**, beings of pure creative will who crystallised chaos into the blocks of Vaelthar. One of them inverted — the Forge inside it began to consume rather than create. |
| **What it wants** | To find the thing it lost. It no longer remembers what that thing is; it only knows the search. |
| **Why it takes people** | Villagers carry a trace of Forge-energy from generations of block-craft. The Hollow absorbs that warmth trying to remember. |
| **Why it is not a cartoon villain** | It is not eating, not conquering, not punishing. It is *searching*. Auren is the first person in three hundred years whose presence it recognises. |

### Act I — The Descent

**World state:** Edhari is crumbling. Ash falls continuously. The dungeon gate is sealed, and Elder Maren is behind it.

**Player goal:** find survivors, reach the gate, and open a way down.

**Narrative through-line:** Auren wakes in the south shelter, walks to the campfire, and hears Maren's voice from the gate. Optional: find **Toma** hiding in the west house. At the gate, Maren explains enough to point Auren east. Auren defeats **Garren** — the former gatekeeper, now a Guard Husk — learns what the village did, and returns to the gate. The teal sigil, dark for three centuries, **blazes for Auren alone**. The gate opens; the Hollow Reach lies beyond.

**Closing image:** Auren steps through. Something enormous draws one slow breath in the dark below. Maren's last line: *"Tell it Maren is still here."*

### Act II — The Truth in the Deep

**World state:** Below Edhari, the architecture is older and clearly pre-human. The Reach is half-built, half-Unravelled.

**Player goal:** find the three villagers taken below, learn what the Hollow was, and gather the Shaper Fragments.

**Narrative through-line:** Auren fights **The Warden** — Renna, a former village elder before Maren — and receives the first Shaper Fragment. The Fragment triggers Auren's first involuntary shaping of a block from nothing. In three Shaper Chambers, Auren rescues **Bracken** (mason), **Sela** (offering-keeper), and **Toma** (the child from Act I). Maren, now speaking through campfires, reveals **the Cradle**: a half-built structure the Hollow has been trying to finish for three hundred years. Auren fights **The Architect** — the Hollow's semi-conscious fragment — inside that unfinished work, and collects Fragments 2 and 3. Beyond it, the Hollow's full form is visible for the first time.

**Closing image:** three survivors asleep by a fire at the edge of an impossibly large chamber; the Hollow barely awake; the choice looming.

### Act III — The Reshaping

**World state:** the Unravelling has reached the Edhari surface. The final campfire burns at the edge of the Hollow's chamber.

**Player goal:** make the choice.

**Ending A — The Final Unravelling:** Auren drives the Shaper Fragments into the Hollow, forcing it to unmake itself. Edhari stabilises; the Forge goes dark. The world is safe but diminished. *"We are free of gods. Now the rest is up to us."*

**Ending B — The Reshaping:** Auren uses the Fragments to complete the unfinished cradle. The Hollow remembers enough to become dormant; Edhari is rebuilt from below. The sky turns golden; something immense still breathes below. *"We built it back together. I don't know what that means yet."*

## 2. Auren's Journey

### What Auren wants

| Stage | Want | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Act I | Survive; find survivors; kill what did this | Immediate, visceral reaction to the ruin |
| Act II | Understand the creature; recover the taken | Maren gives a mission; Auren's stubbornness carries it |
| Act III | Decide what the world becomes next | The only one the gate opened for; the only one who can choose |

### What stands in the way

- **Their own body.** Stamina, dodging, and death teach Auren their limits before they teach their power.
- **The Unravelling.** Structure is unstable where the Hollow is near; the world itself resists passage.
- **Partial truth.** Maren withholds out of care, not deception. Auren must see enough to believe.
- **Moral weight.** The enemy is not evil; it is lost. Destroying it is not a triumph without cost.

### How Auren changes

**Survivor → Warrior → Shaper**

- *Survivor:* hesitant, alert, untrusting of their own strength.
- *Warrior:* fights with confidence, carries a mission, accepts death as a door.
- *Shaper:* by Act II's end, Auren shapes blocks from nothing involuntarily; by Act III, they do it deliberately. The mechanical progression (dodge → attack → build → shape) mirrors the emotional arc exactly.

### The first spark

When Auren approaches the dungeon gate in Act I, the teal sigil blazes in response to them and no one else. The game does not comment. Players who replay will catch it.

## 3. Principal Cast (5)

| Character | Role | Voice | Relationship to Auren |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Elder Maren** | Village record-keeper; keeper of the seal and the old stories | Elderly, urgent but never panicked; sparse, weighted; withholds truth out of care | Guide and witness. She suspects what Auren is before Auren does. In Act II she speaks through campfires because the gate is sealed behind Auren. |
| **Toma** | Child of Edhari; artist of the west-house drawing; one of three Act II survivors | Small, frightened, very few words; notices the wrong details (calls the Hollow *"the tall one"*) | Human stake. Auren promises to come back up for them, but the descent pulls Auren down instead. Toma is ordinary — not Shaper-touched — which is why the Hollow's tragedy is measured against them. |
| **Bracken** | Village mason; owner of the dropped chisel in Act I; Act II survivor | Blunt, practical, deflects shock into craft-talk; names stone before he names fear | Fellow survivor Auren frees. He is the evidence that people were taken mid-work, not killed outright. |
| **Sela** | Keeper of the offering rite; the source of the "echo" voice at the well; Act II survivor | Ritual cadence even in ordinary speech; comfortable with not-knowing in a way Maren is not | Connection to the village's centuries of offerings. She kept the rite without ever knowing if it was answered. |
| **The Hollow** | The last Shaper; grief-inverted creative force; the antagonist | No dialogue in Acts I–II. It is present through ash, teal light, murals, and the sound of something searching | Not a villain to defeat but a tragedy to judge. Auren is the first person in three hundred years whose presence it recognises. |

### Cast notes

- **Maren** is only ever seen as shadow and one grey hand through the gate crack in Act I; her full design exists for later reveals.
- **Toma's toy** deliberately carries no teal. It is the one block-crafted object in the world with no Forge-light — innocence performing a diminished echo of a divine act.
- **Bracken** and **Sela** were unnamed in earlier bibles; they are named now in the shipped `assets/story/act2.json` (2026-08-14) and fill the "three survivors, one of them Toma" gap without altering any LOCKED fact.
- **The Warden** (Renna) and **The Architect** are enemy escalations, not members of the speaking cast. Renna is a former village elder; the Architect is the Hollow's semi-conscious fragment wearing the shape of an unfinished Shaper-form.

## 5. Alignment with Existing Game Code & Data

This bible is written against the shipped story data and the current implementation. It does not invent new lore threads; it names and orders what already exists.

### Story data files

- `assets/story/act1.json` — source of truth for Act I quests, dialogue, lore items, regions, and the `act_end` sequence (`client/src/quest.rs:118` loads these definitions).
- `assets/story/act2.json` — source of truth for Act II quests `q6_the_warden` through `q12_one_slow_breath`, plus NPCs Bracken and Sela, and code-spawned entities `the_warden`, `the_architect`, `stasis_bracken`, `stasis_sela`, `stasis_toma`.
- `docs/act1-script.md` and `docs/act2-script.md` — human-readable schema contracts and author copy.

### Maps / locations

- `maps/edhari.json` — Act I map. All Act I coordinates were verified against this file (2026-07-31). Named regions include `spawn_shelter`, `campfire_square`, `village_road`, `well_square`, `west_house`, `east_house`, `gate_square`, `guard_post_east` (code-spawned), and `hollow_reach_intro` (code-spawned).
- `maps/hollow_reach.json` — Act II map. **Does not exist yet** (Shiba lane). All Act II coordinates in `act2.json` are provisional until the map lands.

### Quests in code

- The quest runtime is in `client/src/quest.rs` (`QuestDef` at `:118`, `QuestJournal` at `:310`, objective kinds at `:237`).
- Act I quest IDs referenced directly in code: `q1_embers` (`:694`), `q3_gatekeeper` (`:859`), `q4_what_walls_remember` (`:1744`), `q5_sigil_that_knew_you` (`:1845`).
- Act II starts at `q6_the_warden` via `act1.json`'s `act_end` reward `"unlock_act": "act2"`.

### Enemies

- **Guard Husk / Garren** — the first enemy, implemented in `client/src/combat.rs` (`spawn_guard_husk` at `:1507`) and `client/src/characters.rs` (`Who::Garren` at `:67`). AI state machine in `client/src/combat.rs` (`HuskState` at `:867`). This is the canon first enemy.
- **The Warden** (`the_warden`) — former village elder Renna, almost fully Unravelled; the first chapter boss. Listed as a code-spawned combat entity in `assets/story/act2.json` (`entities` block).
- **The Architect** (`the_architect`) — the Hollow's semi-conscious fragment; the second chapter boss. Also a code-spawned combat entity in `assets/story/act2.json`.
- **Note on redesign proposals:** `client/src/enemies.rs` contains unshipped replacement silhouettes (`Reaver`, `Sentinel`, `Stalker`). The story bible keeps the locked ladder of **Guard Husk → Warden → Architect** until those redesigns are adopted by the combat lane.

### NPCs and rescue targets

- Roster NPCs in `act1.json`: `maren`, `echo`, `toma`.
- Added in `act2.json`: `bracken`, `sela`.
- Code-spawned rescue targets in `act2.json`: `stasis_bracken`, `stasis_sela`, `stasis_toma`.

### World rules already in code/data

- **Campfire = Forge-point / rest + respawn anchor.** Implied by the campfire objective in `q1_embers` and the `survive_death` objectives.
- **Death is not permanent.** Player respawns at the last campfire with full enemy respawn and no item loss (`docs/first-five-minutes.md` §Act 3; `client/src/combat.rs` respawn flow).
- **The Teal is Shaper-light.** `#4FC9D6` appears only on Shaper-origin objects: the gate sigil, Shaper Fragments, Warden/Architect cracks. Village-native characters and objects carry no teal.
- **Player-placed blocks persist.** Act I already asks the player to place one block to bridge the collapsed gap (`q4_what_walls_remember` `o1_build`).

## 7. Known Discrepancies & Notes

1. **The Warden's act placement.** `docs/first-playable-loop.md` and earlier versions of this bible describe The Warden as Act I's chapter boss. The shipped `act1.json` vertical slice ends at the gate opening (`q5_sigil_that_knew_you`); The Warden fight ships mechanically as `q6_the_warden` in `act2.json`. Narratively it remains the closing beat of the Descent.

2. **Block placement timing.** `docs/first-playable-loop.md` states building is introduced in Chapter 2, but `act1.json` `q4_what_walls_remember` already teaches `place_block` to cross the collapsed gap. This beat was moved earlier to support the back-way traversal to the gate.

3. **Enemy redesign proposals vs. shipped archetype.** `client/src/enemies.rs` proposes `Reaver`/`Sentinel`/`Stalker` replacement silhouettes, but `client/src/combat.rs` still spawns the **Guard Husk**. The story canon stays with Guard Husk/Warden/Architect until the combat lane adopts the redesigns.

4. **Act II map not built.** `maps/hollow_reach.json` does not exist; all `act2.json` coordinates are provisional.

5. **Act III not yet written.** Only the ending choice is defined; quest data for Act III does not exist.

6. **q4 `o1_build` runtime issue.** The `place_block` objective in `act1.json` is authored correctly, but the current demo path can fail to register block placement under certain map-column conditions (see runtime notes in `docs/first-five-minutes.md` and the working-tree `R-key place_block unreachable` memo). This is an implementation/qa issue, not a lore discrepancy.
