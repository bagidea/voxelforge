# Fill rig v2 — every light value that moved, and why (2026-08-14)

Lane: fill / shadow (`client/src/look.rs`). Author: poppy.
A/B switch: `VOXELFORGE_LOOK_GEN=v1` (before) vs unset (after, shipped).

## The one-line reason

The rig had **one key light and one flat `AmbientLight`**. `AmbientLight` gives every
surface in open shade the same irradiance regardless of which way it faces — and a
voxel scene is nothing *but* axis-aligned faces, so orientation is the only shape cue
a cube has and a flat fill spends all of it. That is the "flat" the reference shaders
don't have: in `_poppy_lookv2/ref/complementary-style.png` a grass block's top face is
plainly brighter *and* cooler than its own side face, everywhere in frame, including
deep in shadow where no sun reaches either one.

So the fill was split into a **cool near-zenith sky fill** and a **warm shallow ground
bounce**, and the flat term was cut to a floor.

## Values

### Day (`Hour::GOLDEN` — the const `hour()` returns unless `VOXELFORGE_LOOK_NIGHT`)

| knob | v1 (before) | v2 (after) | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ambient_lux` (flat) | 2200 | **1150** | Floor only. The missing 1050 did not vanish — it went directional. |
| `sky_fill` | — | **[0.62, 0.76, 1.00]** | Cool: stands in for the sky, which is blue even at 22° sun (`Hour::sky` = [0.36, 0.60, 0.90]). Paler than the sky itself so tops read "lit by sky", not "painted blue". |
| `sky_fill_lux` | — | **1400** | The half that lands on horizontal faces. |
| `SKY_FILL_ELEV` | — | **76°** | Not 90°: a vertical fill leaves every vertical face at exactly zero, and `looking_to` degenerates as the direction approaches `Vec3::Y`. 76° keeps `cos 76° = 0.24` on sides, 0.97 on tops — the split without a black side. |
| `bounce` | — | **[1.00, 0.78, 0.50]** | Warm: it is the sun's own light returning off ground the sun already tinted (amber grass/dirt at this hour). |
| `bounce_lux` | — | **1100** | |
| `BOUNCE_ELEV` | — | **28° below horizon** (travels up) | Shallow on purpose. A straight-up bounce only reaches undersides, and undersides are a small share of a voxel frame. At 28° the horizontal term (`cos 28° = 0.88`) dominates, so what it lifts is the **lower half of sun-facing walls** — the single most visible thing the flat fill was standing in for. |

### What that does to irradiance, per surface, in shade

**ANALYTIC, NOT MEASURED.** Every number in this table is hand-computed
`lux × cos(angle between the fill direction and the face normal)`. It is the
*intent* of the rig, read off the constants — it is **not** read off any rendered
frame, and it is not evidence. The measured evidence is the plate set and the gate
table at the bottom of this doc; if the two ever disagree, the frame wins.

The budget is roughly conserved on the surface that had the most of it, and
deliberately **not** conserved on the ones that should never have had as much:

| surface (in shade) | v1 flat | v2 flat + sky + bounce | total |
|---|---|---|---|
| shaded ground | 2200 | 1150 + 1358 + 0 | **2508** |
| wall, shadow side | 2200 | 1150 + 339 + 0 | **1489** |
| wall, sun side | 2200 | 1150 + 0 + 971 | **2121** |
| underside | 2200 | 1150 + 0 + 516 | **1666** |

Under v1 all four were the *same number*. That row is the whole bug.

### Night (`Hour::NIGHT`)

`ambient_lux` 90 → **42**, `sky_fill_lux` **62** @ 76°, `bounce_lux` **16** @ 28°
(`sky_fill` [0.44, 0.58, 0.98], `bounce` [0.55, 0.56, 0.68] — the bounce goes nearly
neutral at night because there is no warm sun left to return).

### Shadow edge

| knob | v1 | v2 | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PCSS_WIDTH` | 16.0 | **8.0** | From the ladder already in the file: 8.0 gives 7.5 px mean / **5.0 px median** vs 4.3/4.0 with PCSS off. A median that barely moves while the mean climbs is the wanted shape — contact edges (blocker sitting *on* receiver) stay sharp, only edges with a real blocker-to-receiver gap open up. 16.0 (11.2 mean / **9.0 median**) moves the median too, i.e. it softens edges that should have stayed crisp. |
| PCSS tier gate | `Ultra` only | **`High` and up** | `LookQuality::default()` is High, so at v1 the *player* saw no penumbra at all while the one plate that did run Ultra saw a penumbra so wide it read as blur. Both halves are the same mistake: the knob was never judged at the tier it ships at. |

Related, same patch: `quality_from_env()` is now public and `SettingsPlugin` reads it,
because `insert_resource` from settings.json was landing *after* `LookPlugin` and
silently overwriting `VOXELFORGE_LOOK_QUALITY` — from 8176d01 every `--play` shoot ran
at the saved tier no matter what the script exported. That is why "grade-vista at Ultra"
was shooting inside its own PCSS-off noise floor.

## What is NOT mine in this diff

`look.rs`'s uncommitted diff also carries the **atmosphere / haze re-measure**
(`Atmosphere` + `AtmosphereSettings` + `ScatteringMedium`, `HAZE_FULL` 150 → 72,
`HAZE_START` 20 held) from the sky lane, off flamingo's depth ruler. I did not author it
and I claim no credit for it. It matters here only in that `LookGen` **does not fork
it** — the haze and the atmosphere are byte-identical in both arms of my A/B, so they
cannot inflate the before/after. The GEN switch forks the fill lights and PCSS, nothing
else.

### The four gates, and the proof each one actually fails

A gate that prints is not a gate. Each of these was run against a deliberately
wrong input before being trusted with the real plates:

| gate | what it refuses | proven by |
|---|---|---|
| **1 freshness** | an exe not newer than `_poppy_fillrig_build.cmd`, i.e. a leftover from an earlier retry. `target-poppy` is reused across retries and a `0xc0000142` DLL-init death writes **no** `^error` line, so "0 errors" alone cannot clear a build. Also requires `EXIT=0`. | pointed `BIN` at `_flamingo_before.exe` (14:02) → `GATE1 FAIL … Refusing to shoot it`, **exit 2** |
| **2 provenance per arm** | a frame whose own `LOOK_FILL` line does not match the arm that asked for it (`gen=V1 ambient=2200 sky=0lux` / `gen=V2 ambient=1150 sky=1400lux`). | stub binary that always claims V2 → **3 GATE2 FAILs**, one per v1 arm; the v2 arms passed |
| **3 arms differ** | v1 and v2 coming back byte-identical, i.e. a dead env hook under a "look how much better" caption. | same stub (identical PNG both arms) → **3 GATE3 FAILs** |
| **4 shade-band sigma / p05 / key drift** | sigma rising < +10 % (no top/side split), p05 falling > 2.0 (shade re-crushed), sunlit-p90 drift > 8.0 (exposure walked off). | identical pair → `FAIL sigma +0% (need +10%)`, `VERDICT: FAILED`, **exit 1** |

The key-band gate is a **ceiling, not a zero**: `bounce_lux` at 28° is *supposed* to
land on sun-facing walls, so some rise there is the feature working. 8.0 L bounds a
runaway exposure; the 2.0 warn line is already ~5× the 0.28–0.39 repeat-shot floor
`look.rs` measured for this capture path.

- Plates + numbers: `_poppy_fillrig/` (`fillrig_before_after.png` + the six PNGs and
  their logs). Nothing here is claimed as "looks better" on anything but those plates.
