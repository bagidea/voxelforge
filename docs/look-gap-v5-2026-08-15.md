## The table

`scripts/_poppy_lookv3_pairs.py` over `docs/assets/look/`. Estimators, so the
columns are not read as something they are not:

- **p05 / p50 / p95** — Rec.709 luma percentiles. p05 is the shade floor.
- **warmth** — mean `R − B` over the midtone band `[p35, p75]`.
- **micro** — local contrast, `mean |pixel − 3×3 mean|`. A bloom veil drives it down.
- **orient** — *named* top-vs-side face separation; the function actually computes
  a tonal spread (brightest-quartile median − darkest-quartile median) and does
  no gradient filtering at all. Kept as-is because v3/v4/v5 were all graded with
  it and a corrected estimator would not be comparable. Flagged, not silently
  swapped. See the naming note in `scripts/_poppy_lookv5_gate.py`.

## What the three moves bought

The round's headline change was three constants moved together:

| constant | v4 | v5 | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `IBL_NITS_DAY` | 440 | 330 | give back ~210 lux of cool directional fill |
| `AMBIENT_LUX_V3` | 380 | 620 | take ~240 lux back in `Hour::ambient`, the rig's warmest light |
| `IBL_NITS_NIGHT` | 23 | 14 | night's env map is blue in BOTH halves; scaling it scales blue |

(Also in the same commit and measured in the same numbers: `IBL_HORIZON_MIX`
0.55 → 0.68, `RIM_COLOR` R 0.78 → 0.86, `RIM_LUX_NIGHT` 30 → 20,
`CONTACT_SHADOW_LENGTH_V3` 1.10 → 0.85, and the v3-only grade ramp —
`SHADOW_GAIN_V3` 1.08, `MIDTONE_CONTRAST_V3` 1.18, `HIGHLIGHT_CONTRAST_V3` 1.20,
`HIGHLIGHT_GAIN_V3` 0.98. The three above are the ones the round was designed
around; the rest are not free of the results below and are not claimed to be.)

After-plate deltas, v4 (`59a76ae7`, `_poppy_lookv4_chain.log`) → v5 (`207ce021`):

| scene | p05 | p50 | p95 | warmth | orient |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| outdoor-noon | 48.47 → **37.97** (−10.50) | 93.01 → 88.64 | 141.75 → 141.03 | 78.28 → **87.65** (+9.37) | 65.62 → 71.99 |
| evening-raking | 18.68 → **21.56** (+2.88) | 71.96 → 72.56 | 120.60 → 119.58 | 73.26 → **74.81** (+1.55) | 74.94 → **70.46** (−4.48) |

**The shade floor is the clause the round won, on all three plates.** Evening and
night were both under the 20.40 bar on the v4 plates (18.68 and 19.63) and both
clear it now (21.56 and 21.77). Noon's p05 dropping 10.5 points is a gain, not a
loss: its defect was range, and p95/p05 goes 2.92 → 3.71 against the reference's
7.59 — still the worst of the three, now moving the right way. Evening 6.45 →
5.55 and night 7.92 → 7.41 both moved *toward* the reference from the other side.
`micro` is up on every plate (3.059→3.895, 2.150→2.722, 1.286→1.393), so the
extra floor did not arrive as a veil.

## Warmth is NOT back to baseline — two of three scenes still fail

Stated plainly, because this is the clause the brief joined with AND:

| scene | v2 baseline | v5 after | delta | clause |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| outdoor-noon | 79.03 | **87.65** | **+8.62** | **PASS** |
| evening-raking | 79.01 | **74.81** | **−4.20** | **FAIL** |
| night-firelit | 43.48 | **33.66** | **−9.82** | **FAIL** |

The day fix worked and worked hard — noon is now 8.6 points *warmer* than its own
v2 baseline, which is more than the brief asked for. It did not carry to the
other two hours, and night went **backwards against the v4 plate** (37.50 → 33.66)
even though `IBL_NITS_NIGHT` was pulled back specifically to recover it. 9 → 23 →
14 nits traces warmth 37.50 → 30.79 → 33.66 and floor 19.63 → 23.41 → 21.77: that
term trades warmth for floor monotonically, in both directions, and 14 is a
midpoint, not a fix. **Night bought +2.14 of floor with −3.84 of warmth.**

Two things that gate says which the pair-check above does not:

1. **Warmth fails 2 of 3.** Above.
2. **Separation regressed on evening-raking** — 71.66 before vs 70.46 after, so
   the plate the whole rig is graded on now separates *worse* than v2. On the v4
   plates that same scene was 71.62 → 74.94, i.e. this is new in v5 and the
   likely payer is `CONTACT_SHADOW_LENGTH_V3` 1.10 → 0.85 plus the softer grade
   toe. It was not part of the brief I was tuning against and I did not notice it
   until the gate was run for this write-up.

### One caveat on reading small deltas

The v2 `before` plate is supposed to be fixed — every constant v5 moved is behind
`v3()` (verified: `IBL_NITS_*`, `IBL_HORIZON_MIX`, `AMBIENT_LUX_V3`, `RIM_COLOR`,
`RIM_LUX_NIGHT`, all reached only inside `if v3()` blocks, and `ibl_env()` returns
`None` outright under v2). It still moved between rounds: noon p05 49.70 → 48.65,
noon warmth 79.21 → 79.03, night warmth 43.10 → 43.48, and the `_before.png` files
changed size. So ~1 luma unit and ~0.4 warmth of the numbers above is round-to-
round drift, not signal — most likely other lanes' uncommitted changes to
`scene.rs` / `enemies.rs` / `anim.rs` compiled into the same binary. It does not
touch the −4.20 and −9.82 conclusions, which are 10× and 25× that. It does mean
evening's +1.55 warmth gain is within shouting distance of noise.

## v6 — where the warmth gets bought back

Not run. Plan only.

**The rule v5 proved: warmth has to come from a term whose COLOUR is a free
parameter, not from one whose level is.** Every lux v5 moved was moved through a
light whose hue was already fixed by `Hour`, which is why the day fix landed
(`Hour::ambient` at GOLDEN is sRGB [0.96, 0.90, 0.48], the warmest thing in the
rig) and the night one could not (`Hour::sky_fill` and `Hour::bounce` at NIGHT are
B > G > R in both halves — documented on `IBL_NITS_NIGHT`, and no size of that
constant changes it). So v6 spends on hue directly, one lever per failing scene,
and does not touch the shade floor that v5 just paid for. **Night** takes the
biggest deficit (−9.82) and gets the cheapest fix: `AMBIENT_LUX_V3_NIGHT` is 14
lux against day's 620, and it is the only flat, warm-capable term at that hour —
raising it (and warming `Hour::ambient` at NIGHT, which currently has no warm
duty because the campfire was assumed to cover it) adds R−B *and* p05 in the same
move, where night has ~1.4 points of floor headroom to spare. **Evening**'s −4.20
is most likely the day kicker: `RIM_LUX` 600 is unchanged, unshadowed, and lands
on every face turned within 90° of it — and since evening's separation went DOWN
to 70.46 anyway, that kicker is no longer buying the thing it costs warmth for.
Make `RIM_LUX` hour-dependent and trim it at GOLDEN only, rather than pushing
`RIM_COLOR` R past 0.86, which would collapse the B > G > R ordering that makes
the edge read as a different light. **Both**, if those two land short, are backed
by a v3-only white balance: `grade::TEMPERATURE` is shared with v2 and cannot move
without moving the before plate, so it needs a `TEMPERATURE_V3` added exactly the
way `MIDTONE_CONTRAST_V3` was — it is the only knob that acts on R−B directly and
costs zero light. It goes last, not first: it is global, and noon is already +8.62
over its own baseline, so a global warm offset sized for night would push noon
further past the reference rather than toward it. Alongside all of that, v6 owes
two things that are not tuning: the evening separation regression above needs an
owner before more shade-floor spending, and the `orient` estimator needs its
promised gradient filter plus a re-baseline of v3/v4/v5 — it is currently grading
a clause it does not measure.
