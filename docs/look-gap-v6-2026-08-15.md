# Look v6 — the round that found the payer, and it was not the one v5 named

Poppy, 2026-08-15. Round record for the v6 constant move in `client/src/look.rs`.
Companion to `docs/look-gap-v5-2026-08-15.md` (the round before, whose §"v6 —
where the warmth gets bought back" is the plan this round was run against).

The headline is not the gate result. It is that **v5's plan named the wrong lever
on two of its three clauses, and the round only landed because the plan was
measured before it was baked** instead of after.

## How this round was run, and why that is the finding

v5 spent one 20-minute relink per candidate and sized three constants off one
shot each. v6 spent one relink total. Every lever the plan called for already had
an env hook that `hour()` / `rim_lux()` / `grade_knobs()` read *before* the
shipped constant:

| plan lever | hook it was swept through |
|---|---|
| `AMBIENT_LUX_V3_NIGHT` | `VOXELFORGE_LOOK_AMBIENT=<lux>` |
| `Hour::ambient` at NIGHT | `VOXELFORGE_LOOK_LIGHT=kr,kg,kb,ar,ag,ab` |
| `RIM_LUX` | `VOXELFORGE_LOOK_RIM=<lux>` |
| `grade::TEMPERATURE` | `VOXELFORGE_LOOK_GRADE=temp,sat,mid,hi_gain` |

So 55 candidate frames were shot on the **v5 exe** (`207ce021…`, the one that
shot the v5 plates), scored against the same three clauses the gate grades, and
only the winner was baked and relinked. `scripts/_poppy_lookv6_sweep.sh` +
`scripts/_poppy_lookv6_measure.py`; the estimators are *imported* from
`_poppy_lookv3_pairs.py`, never re-typed.

**The first row of every sweep is `ctrl`, with no overrides at all.** Against the
committed v5 after-plate:

| night-firelit | p05 | warmth | spread |
|---|---|---|---|
| committed `_after.png` (v5 doc) | 21.77 | 33.66 | 91.13 |
| `ctrl`, re-shot through the sweep harness | 21.78 | 33.89 | 91.13 |

That is the whole licence to trust the other 54 rows: the hook reaches the
shader, and re-shoot noise is **≤ 0.23 on warmth, ≤ 0.01 on p05, 0.00 on
spread**. Every margin claimed below is quoted against that.

## What the plan got wrong

### ② The evening kicker is not the payer. Dimming it makes evening *colder*.

v5's plan: "`RIM_LUX` 600 is unchanged, unshadowed, and lands on every face
turned within 90° of it… make `RIM_LUX` hour-dependent and trim it at GOLDEN
only." One binary, `evening-raking`, only `VOXELFORGE_LOOK_RIM` moving:

| rim lux | 600 (shipped) | 420 | 300 |
|---|---|---|---|
| warmth | 74.06 | **73.93** | 74.45 |

The bar is 79.01. Trimming the kicker moves warmth by less than half the noise
floor and costs separation for it. The reasoning was sound — it *is* a cool
unshadowed light — and it was simply too small to matter: 600 lux against the
~2500 lux a shaded face receives, on a frame whose midtone band is mostly lit
ground. **`RIM_LUX`, `RIM_COLOR` and `RIM_LUX_NIGHT` are untouched by v6.**

Every other light-side lever came back inside a point, on the same binary:

| evening lever | move | warmth gain |
|---|---|---|
| `AMBIENT_LUX_V3` | 620 → 1150 | +1.40 |
| sky-fill / bounce split | 1400/1100 → 800/1700 | +1.34 |
| `RIM_LUX` | 600 → 300 | +0.39 |
| `grade::TEMPERATURE` | 0.05 → 0.07 | **+6.62** |

### ③ `TEMPERATURE_V3` was the plan's last resort. It is the only thing that works.

The plan put it last because it is global and noon was already +8.62 over its own
baseline. That ordering is why v5 shipped short: on `evening-raking` a white
balance is not one lever among several, it is the *only* one that clears the bar.
Baked as `grade::TEMPERATURE_V3 = 0.07`, v3-only — the shared `TEMPERATURE` moves
the v2 before-plate too, which would move the bar and the reading together and
measure nothing.

**The ceiling was re-measured, because that constant's own note demands it.** The
0.05 ceiling was set on a 2026-08-01 frame running `POST_SATURATION` 1.90, and
the note says do not raise it without re-running `scripts/colour_gate.py` on a
real frame. Four sky-facing frames on this binary (`_poppy_lookv6/sky`,
`VOXELFORGE_CINE` look-at raised to y=30 so the gate has sky to read):

| temperature | 0.05 | **0.07** | 0.08 | 0.09 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gate A magenta fraction | 0.01 % | **0.05 %** | 23.62 % | 69.38 % |
| colour gate | PASS | **PASS** | **FAIL** | **FAIL** |

0.07 sits 40× under the 2 % bar; 0.08 is over it by 12×. The onset is that
sharp, which is why this is 0.07 and not 0.075, and why the next round does not
get to nudge it without shooting that sweep again.

**Disclosed, not glossed: Gate B SKIPPED on all four frames.** It reads the flat
`ClearColor`, which the sky dome replaced, so the check that originally caught
the 2026-08-01 magenta cast is structurally unmeasurable now and Gate A is
carrying it alone. That is a gap in the evidence, not a pass.

### The evening separation regression had an owner, and it was the grade

v5 shipped `evening-raking` separating worse after than before (71.66 → 70.46)
and guessed `CONTACT_SHADOW_LENGTH_V3` paid for it. It did not.
`MIDTONE_CONTRAST_V3` — raised 1.12 → 1.18 in the *same* commit — is monotone
against the spread estimator, in the opposite direction to the one the 1.18 note
assumes. One base, night-firelit, only this knob moving:

| midtone contrast | 1.50 | 1.42 | 1.34 | 1.26 | **1.18** | **1.10** | 1.00 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| spread | 70.53 | 75.99 | 80.92 | 85.16 | **89.32** | **93.07** | 99.08 |

Shot again on the shipped v5 rig instead of the warm-ambient base, the ladder
reads 82.75 / 91.13 / 94.99 / 100.83 at 1.34 / 1.18 / 1.10 / 1.00 — the slope
belongs to the constant, not to the base. Bevy grades in sections, and on these
plates the p25 band the estimator takes its dark median from sits inside the
midtone section while the p75 band has already crossed into the highlights, so
pushing midtone contrast up lifts the dark end of the measurement and leaves the
bright end alone.

1.18 → **1.10** gives back +4.02 of evening separation and +3.75 of night
separation, and it is the only lever measured this round that adds separation
**without spending the shade floor** (p05 21.69 → 21.57 evening, 21.78 → 21.77
night). Not 1.00, which measures better still: 1.00 is no midtone contrast at
all, and the p50 argument the 1.18 note makes is still true.

### ① Night: the plan was right, and the reason it had never worked is one line

The plan called for `AMBIENT_LUX_V3_NIGHT` + a warm `Hour::ambient` at NIGHT.
Correct — and the second half is why every previous attempt at the first half
failed. 14 lux of `Hour::NIGHT`'s own ambient is 14 lux of sRGB [0.42, 0.52,
0.78], R−B −0.36, so **raising that constant buys blue**:

| night, 1 binary | lux 14 (shipped) | lux 30, authored colour | lux 26, warm colour |
|---|---|---|---|
| warmth | 33.89 | **31.93** | **46.26** |

`AMBIENT_COLOR_V3_NIGHT = [1.00, 0.62, 0.20]` is the fire's own bounce, one step
deeper than GOLDEN's ground bounce because it comes off ground lit by a small
orange source rather than by the sun. v3-only via `hour()`, *not* by editing
`Hour::NIGHT` — v2 runs that same field at 42 lux, three times the v3 level, so
warming the shared constant would have warmed the bar harder than the reading.

26 lux and not 50, which measures warmer: the flat term is the separation tax
(50 lux → warmth 48.68 but spread 87.02, under the 90.79 bar; 80 lux → 85.16). A
flat light gives every face the same number, which is the one thing a night frame
lit by a single campfire must not do.

## The five constants

| constant | v5 | v6 | what it bought |
|---|---|---|---|
| `grade::TEMPERATURE_V3` | *(new — shared 0.05)* | **0.07** | evening warmth, +6.62 |
| `grade::MIDTONE_CONTRAST_V3` | 1.18 | **1.10** | separation, +4.02 evening / +3.75 night |
| `grade::HIGHLIGHT_GAIN_V3` | 0.98 | **1.06** | evening warmth +1.19 **and** floor +0.43 |
| `AMBIENT_LUX_V3_NIGHT` | 14 | **26** | night warmth + floor |
| `AMBIENT_COLOR_V3_NIGHT` | *(new — shared cool)* | **[1.00, 0.62, 0.20]** | the hue that made the lux work |

`HIGHLIGHT_GAIN_V3` is the one trade taken deliberately against a scene: it costs
noon 0.93 of separation (74.32 → 73.39), which noon has +7.57 of margin for, and
buys the weakest clause on the weakest scene — evening warmth, which has failed
two rounds running — from +1.73 to +2.91.

Untouched, and named because v5's plan or its predecessors reached for all of
them: `RIM_LUX`, `RIM_LUX_NIGHT`, `RIM_COLOR`, `IBL_NITS_NIGHT`, `IBL_NITS_DAY`,
`AMBIENT_LUX_V3`, `CONTACT_SHADOW_LENGTH_V3`, and the shared `grade::TEMPERATURE`.

## What this round still owes

- **`orient` is still misnamed and still ungated.** It is imported as
  `orient` and used as `spread`; its docstring promises a vertical-gradient
  filter and its body computes two percentiles (`flat = l.copy()`). v5 flagged
  it, v6 leaned on it harder than any previous round — the whole
  `MIDTONE_CONTRAST_V3` finding is read through it. It needs the promised filter
  plus a re-baseline of v3/v4/v5/v6, and until then "separation" in this lane
  means "tonal spread", nothing more.
- **Gate B is dead and nothing replaced it.** See above. The magenta check that
  found the original cast cannot run on a sky-dome frame; Gate A is unbacked.
- **The v2 before-plate still drifts round to round** (~1 luma), because other
  lanes' uncommitted `scene.rs` / `enemies.rs` / `anim.rs` changes compile into
  the same binary. Both plates of every pair here come off ONE exe and ONE run,
  so this round's deltas are clean; comparing a v6 number to a v5 number across
  rounds is not.
